Cookie Policy

Last updated: April 2026  ·  Glitz Detailing Ltd

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently and to provide information to website owners. Cookies may be set by the website you are visiting ("first-party cookies") or by third-party services ("third-party cookies").

2. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies on glitzdetailingwickford.co.uk for the following purposes:

  • Essential cookies – necessary for the website to function correctly
  • Analytics cookies – to understand how visitors use our website (via Google Analytics)
  • Marketing cookies – to show you relevant adverts and measure the effectiveness of our Google Ads campaigns

4. Your Consent

When you visit our website for the first time, we will ask for your consent before placing any non-essential cookies (analytics and marketing). You can choose to accept or decline these cookies using the banner at the bottom of the page.

Essential cookies are always active as they are strictly necessary for the website to function and cannot be switched off.

If you decline non-essential cookies, Google Analytics and Google Ads tracking will not be activated on your device.

5. How to Manage or Delete Cookies

You can change your cookie preferences at any time by clicking "Cookie Settings" in the footer of our website, or by managing your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to:

  • View what cookies are stored and delete them individually
  • Block third-party cookies
  • Block cookies from specific websites
  • Block all cookies
  • Delete all cookies when you close your browser

Please note that if you disable cookies, some features of our website may not function correctly.

For information on how to manage cookies in your browser, visit:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Apple Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

6. Google's Use of Data

For more information on how Google uses data when you use our website, visit google.com/policies/privacy/partners. You can also opt out of Google Analytics across all websites by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the cookies we use or for other legal or operational reasons. Please revisit this page regularly to stay informed.
